How We Help

Five structured programs designed to help individuals and families rebuild their lives with dignity.

Housing

Emergency shelter, transitional housing, and homebuyer education for long-term stability.

Learn More →

Education

GED prep, vocational training, financial literacy, and life-skills coaching for reintegration.

Learn More →

Entrepreneurship

Mentoring, business coaching, and seminars from successful entrepreneurs to launch your venture.

Learn More →

Mental Wellness

Stress management, mindfulness, addiction support, and holistic practices for emotional recovery.

Learn More →

Mentoring

Youth mentoring for ages 12–25: GED prep, career exploration, college guidance, and community service.

Learn More →

The Reality Behind Our Mission

Homelessness, domestic violence, recidivism, and foster care aging-out aren't isolated issues — they intersect in the lives of the people we serve. The facts below tell part of the story.

582K+Americans experience homelessness on any given night
25%of homeless adults are veterans or DV survivors
1 in 4aged-out foster youth experience homelessness within 4 years

About Our Founder & Team
Why we do this work — a message from our founder, Natasha Wilson

"I'm a survivor of domestic violence. I relocated to Texas last year with my children and left everything behind to start a new life. I lived in my car for 60 days with my children after money ran out. COHAP Outreach Corporation has assisted me with everything I needed. Natasha Wilson shows so much compassion and is still assisting me to this day."

— Letitia, COHAP Program Participant
