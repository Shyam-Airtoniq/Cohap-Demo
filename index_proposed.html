Breaking the Cycle of Homelessness.
Restoring Hope, One Life at a Time.

COHAP Outreach Corporation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit empowering veterans, domestic violence survivors, formerly incarcerated individuals, and aged-out foster care youth to rebuild their lives through housing, education, and community support.

How We Help

COHAP provides five structured programmes to help individuals rebuild their lives.

Housing

Emergency shelter, transitional housing, and homebuyer education to help individuals and families achieve stable, long-term housing.

Learn More →

Education

GED preparation, vocational training, financial literacy workshops, and life skills building for successful reintegration.

Learn More →

Entrepreneurship

Seminars from successful entrepreneurs, mentoring, business coaching, and support for starting your own business.

Learn More →

Mental Wellness

Stress management, mindfulness, addiction support, and holistic practices for emotional resilience and recovery.

Learn More →

Mentoring

Youth mentoring for ages 17–25, including GED prep, career exploration, college guidance, and community service.

Learn More →
